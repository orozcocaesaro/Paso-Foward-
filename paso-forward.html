Paso Forward
College guidance, peer to peer

Every application starts with one step forward.

Paso Forward is a free, student-run initiative that helps high schoolers and their families navigate the college process — from finding the right-fit schools to writing PIQs, applying for scholarships, and settling into that first week on campus.

How we help

Five steps, one path

We built our support around the actual order families face it in — not a checklist, a walk alongside you.

01

Find your fit

We help students identify schools that actually match their goals, budget, and needs — not just name recognition.

02

Write your PIQs

One-on-one feedback on Personal Insight Questions, so your voice comes through clearly on the page.

03

Apply for aid

Guidance on finding and applying to scholarships that fit your background and story.

04

Talk to real students

Connect directly with current college students who've been through it recently and remember what it felt like.

05

Support your family

Resources for parents too, through decision day and into that first semester away from home.

What's included

Free, all the way through

Applications

School fit & list-building

We help students build a balanced list of reach, target, and safety schools based on what they actually want out of college — not just rankings.

Essays

PIQ & essay support

Structured feedback sessions on Personal Insight Questions and supplemental essays, focused on authenticity over formula.

Financial aid

Scholarship navigation

We help students find scholarships worth their time and walk through the application process step by step.

Mentorship

Conversations with current students

Direct access to college students who can speak honestly about campus life, workload, and what they wish they'd known.

Peer to peer

Advice from people who just did this.

Every mentor in our network is a current college student — close enough to the process to remember exactly what it felt like, and glad to help someone take the next step.

For families

Understanding what your student needs — and what schools are asking of them.

The college process can feel like it's happening in a language you weren't taught. We'll help you understand what your student is going through at each stage, what colleges actually expect, and how to support them without taking over.

During the process

What we help parents with

From junior year through decision day, here's where we can take some of the guesswork off your plate.

Understanding requirements

We'll walk you through what different schools actually require — grades, testing, essays, activities — so you know what your student is working toward.

Reading the timeline

Application deadlines, financial aid forms, decision dates — we'll help you keep track of what's due when, without the panic.

Supporting without pressure

Guidance on how to be involved in a way that helps your student, rather than adding stress to an already stressful year.

Making sense of financial aid

We'll help you understand FAFSA, scholarships, and award letters so you can make informed decisions as a family.

After they leave

Walking through the first week

The process doesn't end at the move-in truck. Here's how we support families once your student is actually on campus.

Move-in day

Saying goodbye

What to expect emotionally — for both of you — and how to make the goodbye feel like a beginning instead of a loss.

Days 1–3

Orientation & syllabus week

What your student is navigating — new routines, roommates, and a campus that doesn't feel like theirs yet.

Days 4–7

Homesickness & check-ins

How often to reach out, what to say, and how to tell the difference between normal adjustment and something that needs more support.

Week 2+

Settling in

What "normal" starts to look like — classes finding a rhythm, friendships forming, and how to keep supporting from a distance.

Ask us anything

Whether it's about requirements, deadlines, or what to expect during move-in week — send us your question and we'll get back to you directly.

For current college students

You just did this. Help someone else take the next step.

Volunteer mentors are the heart of Paso Forward. You don't need to be an expert — just willing to share what you remember about applying, choosing, and adjusting to college, with a student who's a step or two behind you.

Flexible

Give what time you have

Answer a few questions a month or mentor one student consistently through the year — you set the pace.

Real impact

Be the person you needed

Most students say a peer's honest advice mattered more than any guidebook. You can be that person for someone else.

No experience needed

Your story is the qualification

Whatever school you attend, whatever your path looked like — it's useful to someone still figuring theirs out.
